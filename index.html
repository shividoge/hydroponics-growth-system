Analysis & interpretation

What it proves, and where it stops

Engineering significance

  • Non-destructive biomass estimation in controlled environments
  • No destructive harvest needed during growth monitoring
  • A base for autonomous agricultural systems
  • Embedded systems, computer vision and predictive modeling in one framework

Limitations

  • Assumes constant lighting and fixed camera geometry
  • Leaf overlap adds segmentation uncertainty
  • Biomass depends on leaf density and water content
  • Small dataset, n = 6 trials
  • Error sources: edge segmentation, calibration sensitivity, small environmental swings, VOC/CO₂ sensor noise

Where it can go

  • Space-based agriculture (CELSS systems)
  • Autonomous greenhouse optimization
  • Precision agriculture monitoring
  • Resource-constrained environments
